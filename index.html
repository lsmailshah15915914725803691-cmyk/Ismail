Jab aap AI features (chat, voice, image) use karte hain, to aapka message, voice ya image us AI provider ko bheja jata hai jo aapne choose kiya hai (default: Google Gemini), aapki apni API key ke saath. NOVA ke developer ka koi backend server nahin hai, aur NOVA mein abhi koi ads ya analytics SDK nahin hai.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Chat history, app settings and preferences.
  • Your AI API keys. They are encrypted with the Android Keystore and are never sent to the developer.
  • Local activity logs for features you use (for example, Call Assistant activity), and the saved position of the floating avatar.

2. Voice

  • In standby mode, NOVA listens for its wake word using an offline speech engine (Vosk) on your phone.
  • When you start a voice conversation, microphone audio is streamed to Google's Gemini Live service to get responses.

3. Chat, images and screen content

  • Text you type, and images you attach or capture, are sent to the AI provider you selected, using your own API key.
  • If you ask NOVA to look at your screen or act on screen content, that content may be sent to the AI provider so it can answer or plan the next step.

4. Location

If you allow location, NOVA uses your GPS coordinates for weather and location-based answers. Place names are first resolved with Android's built-in geocoder. If that fails, the coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim.

5. Web and video search

Searches in NOVA's built-in browser are sent to DuckDuckGo. YouTube search requests are sent to the YouTube Data API, a Google service. Web pages you open are loaded from their own websites, just like in any browser.

6. Phone permissions and features

NOVA uses these permissions only to perform features on your phone. The developer does not receive this data. When any of this information becomes part of a question you send to an AI provider, that text is sent to that provider.

8. Ads and analytics

NOVA currently shows no advertisements and does not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

9. Children

NOVA is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone stays there until you delete it in NOVA, clear NOVA's storage in Android Settings (Clear data), or uninstall NOVA.
  • Data sent to AI providers, Google, DuckDuckGo or OpenStreetMap is kept by them under their own policies. Contact them directly to request deletion.
  • To stop sending data to a provider, remove its API key in NOVA's API connection settings.

11. Security

API keys are encrypted on your device. Network traffic uses HTTPS or secure WebSocket connections. Please keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

12. Your choices

You can turn off features in NOVA, revoke permissions in Android Settings at any time, and avoid entering any AI API key if you do not want AI processing. A feature that needs a permission stops working when that permission is revoked.

13. International transfers

AI providers and other services may process data in countries other than your own, under their own privacy policies.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of NOVA after an update means you accept the updated policy.
